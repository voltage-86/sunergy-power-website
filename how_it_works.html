How Solar Energy Powers Your Home

Learn how sunlight is converted into electricity.

Step By Step

1

2

3

4

Electricity powers your home

How It Works

Solar panels capture sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC) electricity. An inverter changes this into alternating current (AC), which is used to power your home. Any extra electricity generated can be sent back to the grid.
